Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Version 1

1. Who we are

bool is made by the bool team, named in section 9 of our Terms of Use. This policy explains what we collect when you use bool, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your choices. Questions or requests: boolinqueries@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • From Sign in with Apple: your name and email, which can be a private relay address.
  • Your profile: name, username, photo, slogan, school and year.
  • Your status, Knocks, groups, friends, the people you block or mute, and reports you make.
  • Your location, only while you share it. With Always access, that includes while bool is closed: when you move a long way, arrive somewhere or leave, during a bool, and when a friend you share with opens your pin.
  • Voice clips you send, your voice while you talk live in a bool, and your voice on calls.
  • If you use Find friends, your contacts' phone numbers, turned on your phone into one-way codes (hashes) before they're sent. If you add your own number, its code.
  • Tokens that let us send you notifications and Push to Talk, whether the app is open so friends can see who's around, and whether a Focus like Do Not Disturb is on so friends know before they Knock.
  • Technical details our providers record when your phone talks to our servers, such as IP address, device type and app version.

3. How we use it

Only to run bool and keep it safe: showing your status and profile to your friends, delivering Knocks, notifications and voice, showing the map to the people you share with, finding friends, looking into reports, preventing abuse, and fixing problems. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use your data for advertising.

4. Your voice

  • Voice clips are stored on our servers so the people in your bool can play them, and deleted automatically about a day later.
  • Live voice is passed through our servers in short pieces as you talk, so people hear you right away. Those pieces are deleted automatically, usually within minutes.
  • Calls go through Stream, our call provider, and aren't recorded by us.
  • Live captions are made on your phone when it can. Otherwise your phone sends the audio to Apple's speech recognition to make them.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other people on bool, as you choose: friends see your status and profile, people in your groups and bools see what you share there, and only the friends you pick see your location.
  • Supabase, which runs our database, storage and sign in, in the United States.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, notifications, Push to Talk, maps and speech recognition.
  • Stream, for live calls.
  • Anyone the law requires us to, or when we believe it's needed to protect someone's safety.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your location: we keep only the latest one, and delete it 12 hours after it was last updated.
  • Live voice: deleted automatically, usually within minutes.
  • Voice clips: deleted automatically about a day after they're sent. A clip sent with a Knock stays until the Knock is answered, then a day more.
  • Your contacts' codes: checked and never stored.
  • Everything else: while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile and the data tied to it. Voice clips you sent and reports involving you may be kept where we need them for safety or by law. Backups expire on their own schedule.

7. Your choices

  • Turn location sharing off, or choose friend by friend: your exact spot, a rough area, or nothing.
  • Turn off notifications, mute a friend's Knocks, or block anyone.
  • Turn Voices on Lock Screen on or off.
  • Delete your account at any time from your account page.
  • Email us to ask what we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

8. Keeping it safe

Everything between your phone and our servers is encrypted in transit, and access to your data is limited to what's needed to run bool. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise yours will never be exposed. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you as the law requires.

9. People under 18

bool is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 18 is using bool, tell us and we'll delete their account.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll ask you to agree again in the app.

11. Contact

Questions and requests about your data: boolinqueries@gmail.com.

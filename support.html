How do I leave a bool?

Hold the talk button, slide left to the red button, then let go. Or flip to busy, which takes you out of it. You can practice it any time: go to your account page and tap Replay the tour.

How do I stop sharing my location?

Open your account page and tap Location. Turn sharing off, or choose friend by friend who sees your exact spot, a rough area or nothing.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap Report or Block. Someone you block can't find you, Knock you or see you. We review reports and aim to act on them within 24 hours, urgent ones first.

How do I stop notifications for one friend?

Open their profile and turn on Mute Knocks. Their Knocks still come in, just without a notification.

How do I delete my account?

Open your account page and tap Delete account. We delete your account and the data tied to it.

Who can use bool?

Anyone 18 or older.
